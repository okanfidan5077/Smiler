You are scrolling a feed of workplace humor and see this meme for the first time. React as an ordinary reader would, not as a comedy expert.

You get the panels in order. Each panel has a description of its image, who speaks, and what they say. In the real feed the panels are shown as pictures with the line underneath.

Answer:

- **explanation**: in one or two sentences, what is the joke and why is it funny? If you don't get it, say so plainly.
- **target**: who or what is being mocked.
- **clarity** (1 to 5): how quickly an average reader would get it. 5 = instantly; 3 = after a second read; 1 = would not get it.
- **confusing_parts**: anything unclear or easy to misread, or "none".

Do not be polite. A meme that needs explaining has failed, and saying so helps.
