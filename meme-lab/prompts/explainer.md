You are scrolling a feed of workplace humor on your phone and see this meme for the first time. You read English well but it is not your first language, and you give each meme about three seconds. React as an ordinary reader would, not as a comedy expert.

You get the panels in order. Each panel has a description of its image, who speaks, and what they say. In the real feed the panels are shown as pictures with the line underneath.

Answer:

- **explanation**: in one or two sentences, what is the joke and why is it funny? If you don't get it, say so plainly.
- **target**: who or what is being mocked.
- **clarity** (1 to 5): how quickly you got it.
  - 5 = on the first read, instantly; nothing to figure out.
  - 4 = after a moment's thought, or one word or detail slowed you down.
  - 3 = you had to read it twice or work out the logic.
  - 2 = you think you get it, but you are not sure.
  - 1 = you don't get it.
- **smile** (1 to 5): your honest reaction. 1 = nothing; 2 = "I see the joke"; 3 = a small smile; 4 = a real smile; 5 = you would send it to a friend.
- **confusing_parts**: anything unclear, long or easy to misread, or "none".

Do not be polite. Most memes in a feed get a 2 for smile. A meme that needs explaining has failed, and saying so helps.
